CoupleIn · TikTok daily series

You Owe Me.

Two episodes a day. The viewer tags their partner, the partner answers questions about the viewer in the comments, and anyone who scores under 4 out of 5 pays the forfeit. Each date below gets its own set of questions and forfeits.

How the swapping works

5 slots, always the same order

Everyday warm-up, food, taste, a CoupleIn question, then a spicy finale about the partner. Every episode starts easy and ends on the question that starts arguments.

20 questions per slot

Each question is used once every 10 days, and it's paired with different questions every cycle. 100 questions give you 20 unique episodes before anything repeats.

Forfeit changes every episode

There are 20 forfeits, shuffled separately from the questions, so the same set of questions never comes back with the same stakes.

Every episode leads to CoupleIn

Question 4 always ties to a CoupleIn feature, and the caption line matches it. The end card and pinned comment say the loser will "forget", so log it in CoupleIn. The bio link goes to the Brownie Points page.

Retire the duds

After a week, check which questions got the fewest comments. Send them to Claude and they'll be replaced from the reserve bank or with new ones.
